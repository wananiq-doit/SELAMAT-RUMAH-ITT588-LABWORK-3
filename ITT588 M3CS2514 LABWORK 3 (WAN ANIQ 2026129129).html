Home should be the safest place you know.

Domestic and gender-based violence affects people of every age, background and community in Malaysia. This site helps you recognise the signs, check your situation privately, and find people who will listen.

Who to call in Malaysia

If you are in immediate danger, call 999 first. Search the list below by name or number.

You have legal protection

The Domestic Violence Act 1994 lets a person at risk ask for protection without leaving home first.

Interim Protection OrderCan be issued quickly to stop the abuser from hurting or contacting you while your case is heard.
Protection OrderA court order that sets conditions on the abuser, such as staying away from you or your children.
Free medical careGovernment hospitals have One-Stop Crisis Centres (OSCC) that handle injuries, police reports and counselling together.
